How the Honors section works

The labs are the same as every other section of SCTC 1013. You work them on your own time, and you can bring questions to any instructor's or classroom assistant's office hours. Labs are due Tuesdays at midnight.

Class time is for the arguments. Instead of coaching you through the labs, we use our time together for group activities and discussion — where a result is significant but unimportant, why p-hacking works, what a confidence interval actually claims, and what never makes it into the data at all.

Activity sheets are handed out on paper in class. This page is your map: what we did each day, what it was for, and where the code lives.
