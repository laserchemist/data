🧭 How it works

  1. Your instructor randomly assigns you to one microproject. Open only your assigned notebook below.
  2. Run the first cells, set name, and run my_project(name) to confirm your assignment.
  3. Paste in your Lab 8 toolbox, explore the data, fit and check your line, and dig into the twist.
  4. Make one publication-quality figure and send it to the class gallery with submit_plot(...).
